# GAC Eight-Territory Acceptance

- Full battlefield shows eight territory locations total.
- Your side has two front and two rear territories.
- Opponent side has two front and two rear territories.
- Front territories face the center line.
- Rear territories sit behind their matching fronts.
- Opponent defense circles remain clickable and use existing manual defense editor actions.
- League/format changes alter team capacities inside opponent territories, not the physical eight-territory map.
- Chromium 5v5 remains seven squad defenses plus two fleet defenses.
- Own-side territories do not invent exact squad placement from unavailable/reserved roster data.
- Clicking an own-side territory focuses the existing My Defense roster controls.
