# GAC Full Battlefield Geometry

The GAC battlefield is represented as eight territory locations total: four territories per player.

## Physical map geometry

Your side:
- Rear Top (fleet)
- Front Top (squad)
- Rear Bottom (squad)
- Front Bottom (squad)

Opponent side:
- Front Top (squad)
- Rear Top / Fleet Territory (fleet)
- Front Bottom (squad)
- Rear Bottom (squad)

The two front territories on each side face the center line. Rear territories sit behind their corresponding fronts.

## League requirements

League and format determine the number of defensive teams placed inside the four territories. They do not change the physical eight-territory battlefield geometry.

For example, Chromium 5v5 remains 7 squad defenses plus 2 fleets on each player's defense half. The opponent-side manual circles remain authoritative for visible enemy defense entry.

## Own-side truth boundary

The live roster currently identifies units marked unavailable/on defense but does not expose exact current own-zone placement. The four own-side territory locations therefore do not fabricate squad assignments. Selecting one focuses the existing My Defense roster controls until exact own-zone placement is explicitly entered by the user or exposed by a trusted source.
